# Review agenda: in-person expert review, 2026-10-22

Calls made without a pre-publication gate, under the owner direction of 2026-10-19. Each bullet says what was decided, where it shows on the live site or in the video, and the question for the experts. Append only.

- **Video captions keep "Double Click" in English in every language.** Decided in planning and applied by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. Shows in the caption track of every episode in all ten translated languages, wherever the narration names the series. Question: should the series name stay in English as a brand, or be translated per language?
- **A caption that quotes a guide step heading uses that heading's translation word for word.** Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. Shows in the translated caption tracks whenever the narrator reads out a step heading as it appears in the guide (for example "Next: Select the stations."); ordinary narration that merely uses the same words is translated freely. Question: is matching the on-screen step heading worth the occasional stiff phrasing in spoken captions?
- **The video panel sits above the walkthrough, at most 820px wide, and on the home page between the introduction and the task grid.** Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. Shows at the top of every guide that has an episode, under the guide's title and summary, and as the overview video on the home page, both left-aligned with the text above them. A guide without an episode shows no panel and no gap. Question: is this the right size and place for the video, or should it be larger, smaller or further down the page?
- **Inside the video player, Space and the left and right arrow keys work the video, not the guide.** Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. Shows when a keyboard user has focus on the video or its transcript: Space plays and pauses and the arrows seek, where elsewhere on the page Space starts Play all and the arrows change step. Question: does that split feel natural, or should the guide's step keys always win?
- **Real playback was checked in the WebKit, Firefox and Chromium engines rather than in installed copies of Safari, Firefox and Google Chrome.** Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. The build machine has no Google Chrome or Firefox installed and Safari's remote automation is off, so the 8-second placeholder clip was played through npm run serve in Playwright's WebKit 26.5, Firefox 153 and Chromium 151: picture, sound, captions in two languages, the transcript, keyboard play and the end of the video all worked. Question: when you open a guide with an episode on the live site, does it play, with captions in your language, in the browser you normally use?
- **The documented way to add an episode has no sign-off step: a finished episode goes live with its next deploy.** Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22. Shows in README > Video > Add or replace an episode, which starts from the finished render rather than an approved one, and on the live site, where each guide's video appears as soon as its episode is published, with no waiting for the full set. Question: once the series is complete, should an expert or the owner sign off each new or replaced episode before it is published?
