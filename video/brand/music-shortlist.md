# Music shortlist (gate G5)

**Feature:** PI-ICNT-SW-1083-3 · **Gate:** G5, music licence, in [`../approvals.md`](../approvals.md) · **Version:** v1 · **Record:** [`music.json`](music.json) · **Code:** [`../edit/audio/music_bed.py`](../edit/audio/music_bed.py), [`../edit/mix.py`](../edit/mix.py), [`../edit/audio/loop_points.py`](../edit/audio/loop_points.py)

Every Spatial Adjust video has one music bed under the voice: 3 dB under the VO, ducked a further 9 dB while Brian speaks, mastered with the VO and SFX to -14 LUFS ([`motion-system.md`](motion-system.md), section 9). `video/edit/build.py` re-cuts the bed on its bar grid to each episode's length (the intro, whole bars of a loop, then the outro, with the last chord landed on the end card), so the bed needs a clean intro, a loopable middle and a real ending. One bed serves the whole series: 26 guide episodes, the pilot, and the showcase.

## Excluded: the iCentral Double Click bed

The iCentral MarCom Double Click videos use a **PremiumBeat preview** of the track "Inside the Game", and that licence was never bought. The iCentral licence note (`~/Documents/iCentral_MarCom/06-edit/music-licence.md`, modified 2026-10-05 08:21:39) says: "PREVIEW ONLY. The licence is NOT yet purchased. Do not publish." The file is

`~/Documents/iCentral_MarCom/06-edit/music/candidates-v3/458_full_inside-the-game_0148_preview.mp3`
(modified 2026-10-06 13:40:50, SHA-256 `1ff0f4c425d611c0f9d73d2a5e9fd04de828c8afaad88e4be0f3f7511abdd0b9`)

It cannot be reused, under that name or any other: `mix.py` refuses it by its path, its file name and its SHA-256 ("unlicensed iCentral preview track refused"), along with any other PremiumBeat preview download and any file inside the iCentral folder. Nothing was copied from it; it was only hashed. Option (b) below is the same track **bought with a licence**, which would be a different file (the licensed master, not the watermarked preview).

## Decision

Recorded in [`../approvals.md`](../approvals.md), row G5: **(a), the original Spatial Adjust bed v1**. Decided by the executor under owner direction 2026-10-19; for review in person 2026-10-22.

The owner's direction names the iCentral Double Click bed as the G5 default. That default was not followed, because the bed is the unpurchased preview above: publishing it would breach its licence, and this feature's acceptance criteria require `mix.py` to refuse it. Buying a licence is a purchase only the owner can make, so (b) and (c) wait for the review. (a) needs no licence, covers the series and the showcase outright, and was built to the bar grid the builder cuts on, so finals can be made now.

## If the decision changes

To use (b) or (c) after the review:

1. The owner buys the licence and keeps the receipt or invoice under `<media root>/music/licences/` (never in the repository).
2. Save the licensed full-quality WAV (not the preview) as `<media root>/music/<file>`.
3. Measure its grid: `python3 video/edit/audio/loop_points.py --file <media root>/music/<file> --bpm <n>`, pick the intro end, loop and outro on bar lines, and check them by running `loop_points.py` again on the record.
4. Add a new G5 row to `approvals.md` in the owner's words with the licence number (never edit the decided row), and update `music.json`: `track` (id, title, artist, library, file, sha256, bpm, bar, sections) and `licence` (type, reference = the licence number, scope, term, territories, attribution).
5. Rebuild and remix the episodes: `npm run video -- build ...`, `render`, `mix`.
